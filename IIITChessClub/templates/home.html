About Us

IIIT's Finest
Chess Community

Founded in 2021, we are a vibrant community of chess enthusiasts at IIIT Hyderabad who come together to learn, compete, and celebrate the timeless game of chess.

From beginner-friendly workshops to intense tournaments, our club nurtures players at every level. We proudly host Felicity's largest chess event, where grandmasters play simultaneous matches against students.

  • Monthly Swiss-system tournaments
  • Game analysis & opening workshops
  • Friendly & competitive matches
  • Annual Grandmaster Simul Event
  • ELO-based rating system
